SafeOpt is evaluated with the reference \texttt{safeopt} package, whose \texttt{gp\_opt.py}, \texttt{utilities.py} and \texttt{swarm.py} required Python 3.11 compatibility edits (\texttt{collections.abc} imports); the edits are released with this paper and do not touch the algorithm, but we have not diffed them against the released package line by line. Its GP hyperparameters were chosen on tuning seeds from a 6-configuration grid, smaller than the 18-configuration grid given to our method, which favours our method; the additional safe GP-UCB is our own, more exploratory reimplementation and is not equivalent to SafeOpt.
